## 1. The answer

**Build the off-ramp: a hybrid "muscle & maintenance" membership for adults 40–65 who are on a GLP-1, tapering, or recently stopped.**

Governing idea: **strength is the off-ramp.** The drug is a season; the membership is what comes after it.

- ★ **Mass adoption.** 12% of US adults currently take a GLP-1; 18% have ever; 22% among 50–64-year-olds; women 15% vs men 9% [H1]. Gallup measured a new high in Jul 2026 (11% taking for weight loss; 15% ever) [H2].
- ★ **Most people stop.** 64.8% of users without diabetes discontinue within a year [H4]. Only 8% of obesity patients (no diabetes) are still on therapy at 3 years (14% for Wegovy) [H3]. One claims study: 58% stop within 12 weeks [H5]. Caveat: claims data miss fast-growing cash-pay channels.
- ★ **Then they regain.** BMJ 2026 meta-analysis (37 studies, 9,341 adults): +0.8 kg/month after stopping semaglutide/tirzepatide; back to baseline in ~1.5 years, faster than after behavioral programs; cardiometabolic gains reverse in ~1.4 years [H6]. Other studies agree: STEP-1 extension, two-thirds of the loss regained within a year (2022) [H8]; SURMOUNT-4, +14% body weight within 52 weeks of switching to placebo (2023) [H7]; eClinicalMedicine 2026, ~60% regained within a year [H9].
- ★ **Muscle is part of the loss.** Lean mass is ~31% of weight lost in a 9-trial meta-analysis [H10] and ~25% in a 22-trial one [H11]. DXA "lean mass" ≠ muscle — word claims carefully.
- **The funnel is widening.** Wegovy pill approved 22 Dec 2025 at $149/mo starter doses, $299 higher [H12]; Foundayo (orforglipron) approved 1 Apr 2026 at $149–349 self-pay [H13]; TrumpRx deal: Wegovy $350, Zepbound ~$346, Medicare $50 copay [H14]; Novo cutting US list prices up to 50% from Jan 2027 [H17]. Europe: Wegovy pill approved Jul 2026, on sale in German pharmacies from 1 Sep 2026 at ~€170–280 [H18]; ~9 in 10 UK users buy privately [H19].
- **The gap.** Incumbents earn on prescriptions. WeightWatchers Q2 2026: clinical subscribers 197k (+55.7% YoY) while behavioral subscribers ~2.3m (−24.6% YoY) [H29] — app-only coaching is shrinking. Hims & Hers: 2.89m subscribers, gross margin down from 76% to 64% as branded GLP-1s grew [H30]. Life Time's MIORA clinics are still "in incubation" [H31]. **In-person strength work plus a structured off-ramp is nobody's core business.**

## 3. The pick: the off-ramp membership

### 3.1 Offer (working name: KEEP)

| Tier | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Strength** | $169/mo · $139/mo on 12-month prepay | 2 coached small-group strength sessions/week (≤ 8 people, rented studio or partner gym off-peak) · protein & fibre plan designed by a registered dietitian + monthly group session · strength and body-composition check every 6 weeks (grip, chair-stand, BIA; optional DXA via partner) · 6-month behavioral off-ramp program (dosing stays with the prescriber) · vetted referral list of telehealth/DPC prescribers |
| **Maintain** (digital) | $49/mo | App sessions · nutrition plan · monthly live group · quarterly in-person check |

**Why members stay 12+ months:** the prepay spans the off-ramp, so stopping the drug isn't a reason to cancel; the ~1.5-year regain window [H6] sets the program length; the next assessment is booked at every visit (Neko Health says 75% of customers pre-book their next scan — company-reported [H36]); cohorts create social commitment.

**Capacity math (assumption, not researched):** 8 per class × 2 classes per member per week → one coach running 20 classes/week serves ~80 members ≈ $11–13.5k MRR. Margin depends on local studio and coach costs — test with off-peak rental before signing a lease.

**Women's track:** women use GLP-1s more than men (15% vs 9%) [H1]; WW launched a menopause program from $25/mo (£16.95 UK) in Sep 2025 [H33]. A strength, bone-health and nutrition cohort for women 45–60 that refers HRT to clinicians fits inside KEEP rather than as a separate business.

### 3.2 Price anchors (as indexed, 2026)

- Ro Body: $149/mo, or $74/mo prepaid for a year (third-party tracker, Jun 2026) [H24]
- WW Med+: $74/mo on a 12-month plan after a $25/mo intro; WW's own page shows from $84/mo + $49 consult — promos vary [H25]
- Life Time MIORA continuing care: $149/mo [H26]
- Noom Microdose: $199/mo including medication [H27]
- Drug self-pay: Wegovy pill $149–299 [H12] · Foundayo $149–349 [H13] · Wegovy injection $349 [H15] · Zepbound vials $299–449 [H16] · UK private Mounjaro £140–360 (vendor guide) [H19]

None of the incumbents above includes coached in-person strength training.

### 3.3 Regulatory boundary (general background — get legal advice)

- **A non-clinical operator can offer:** training, general nutrition education, habit coaching, community, non-diagnostic strength and body-composition testing, referrals.
- **Needs licensed clinicians:** diagnosis, prescribing, dosing/tapering/"microdosing" advice, hormone therapy, ordering lab tests (many US states), DXA (some states), individualized medical nutrition therapy (licensed dietitians in many states).
- **Structure:** corporate-practice-of-medicine states bar non-clinicians from controlling physicians. Never pay or accept per-referral fees (anti-kickback / fee-splitting).
- **Claims:** FTC requires competent and reliable evidence for health claims. Say "designed to support", never "prevents muscle loss". Avoid the unregulated "GLP-1 friendly" label [H22].
- **Privacy:** US health apps outside HIPAA fall under the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule; EU/UK health data needs explicit GDPR consent.
- **EU/UK:** prescription medicines can't be advertised to the public — market without naming any drug.
- **Avoid compounded-drug dependence:** Hims moved to branded Wegovy in Mar 2026 [H30].

### 3.4 Risks → fixes

| Risk | Impact | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Telehealth incumbents bundle free coaching | Price pressure on digital tier | Compete on in-person sessions and measured strength gains; make incumbents referral sources |
| People who stop for cost can't pay $169 (about half say the drugs are hard to afford [H1]) | Smaller in-person market | $49 Maintain tier; prepay discount; both cost less than staying on the drug |
| Injury liability | Claims, insurance cost | Certified coaches, pre-participation screening (e.g., PAR-Q+), liability insurance |
| Health-claim exposure | FTC/ASA action | Claims review; no drug names in EU/UK marketing; RD-designed plans |
| Drug price/policy swings | Demand volatility | Value proposition holds whether members are on, tapering or off |

### 3.5 30-day validation sprint

1. Landing page + refundable founding-member deposit in one metro. **Target: 40 deposits.**
2. 15 interviews with GLP-1 users aged 40–65 (on / tapering / stopped). A/B the "off-ramp" vs "keep your muscle" message.
3. Lock 1 studio with off-peak hours, 1 registered dietitian, 2 prescriber referral partners (no per-referral fees).
4. Run a 12-week pilot cohort (8–16 people). **Proceed if** attendance ≥ 75%, strength scores improve, and ≥ 70% intend to renew at month 3.
5. **If it fails:** test the $49 digital tier alone, or move to runner-up #2 or #3.

Targets are judgment calls, not research-derived benchmarks.

## 4. Runners-up

### 4.1 AI Visibility Care Plan (B2B) — best fit for a web/design team

**Trend**
- ChatGPT: 900M weekly users, 50M paying subscribers (Feb 2026) [D1]. Google AI Overviews > 2.5B monthly users; AI Mode > 1B monthly users (Alphabet Q2 2026) [D2].
- Pew: users clicked a result on 8% of visits with an AI summary vs 15% without; 1% clicked a link inside the summary (Jul 2025) [D3].
- Ahrefs: top result gets 34.5% fewer clicks when an AI Overview appears (Apr 2025) [D4]; 58% fewer on Dec 2025 data [D5]. Seer: organic CTR on AIO queries fell from 1.76% to 0.61%; brands cited in the Overview get 35% more organic clicks (Nov 2025) [D6]. Similarweb: zero-click news searches 56% → 69% (May 2024 → May 2025) [D7].
- ★ **Local demand:** 45% of US consumers used AI for local-business recommendations, up from 6% a year earlier; ChatGPT 31%, AI Mode 23% (BrightLocal, Mar 2026, n = 1,002) [D8]. No EU equivalent found.
- **Funding:** Profound $180M Series D at $1.8B (15 Sep 2026), ~7 months after a $96M Series C at $1B (secondary) [D11][D12]; Peec AI (Berlin) $21M Series A, self-reported $4M ARR in 10 months and 1,300+ customers (Nov 2025) [D13]; Scrunch $15M Series A (Jul 2025) [D14]; AthenaHQ $2.7M seed (Jun 2025) [D15].
- **Counter-signal:** AI chatbots still send only ~0.28–0.32% of website traffic [D9][D10]; those visitors stay 68% longer [D10].

**Willingness to pay**
- Tools (monthly; tracked prompts): Otterly $29 / $189 / $489 (15 / 100 / 400) [D16]; Peec $95 / $245 / $495 (50 / 150 / 350) [D17]; Profound $99 (ChatGPT only) / $399 [D18]; Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit $99/domain [D19].
- Agency retainers: $500–1,500/mo monitoring; $2,000–5,000/mo full GEO (agency pricing guides — low reliability) [D20].

**Gap:** tools assume an in-house marketer; agencies start ~$2k/mo. Local and multi-location service firms (clinics, trades, legal) need done-for-you fixes — site structure, schema, reviews/listings, answer-style pages — at a few hundred dollars a month. In the EU, every language/country multiplies the prompts to track.

**Concept:** for service businesses with 1–10 locations. 30–50 local prompts tracked in a white-labelled tool; monthly technical/schema fixes; review and listing hygiene; 2 answer pages/month; share-of-answer report vs 3 competitors; quarterly call. **$390–690/mo (€350–650).** Upsell: hosting/site care and, later, an AI receptionist.

**Risks:** platform citation changes; tools automating the work (Profound's Growth tier already includes articles); hype backlash; never guarantee placement (consumer-protection exposure). Sell share-of-answer plus calls/bookings, not traffic.

### 4.2 Parent Home Check — most durable demand

**Trend**
- 63M US family caregivers in 2025 (~1 in 4 adults; +20M vs 2015); nearly half report a negative financial impact [F1].
- US 65+ population 61.2M in 2024 (+3.1%); older adults outnumber children in 11 states, up from 3 in 2020 [F2]. 4.1M+ Americans turn 65 each year 2024–2027 (industry group) [F3]. EU: 22.0% aged 65+ on 1 Jan 2025 [F4].
- Median owner-occupied US home is 42 years old; ~47% built before 1980; NY median 64, MA/RI 59, TX 28 [F8]. Angi: maintenance spend $2,041 in 2025 (from $1,750); emergency repairs $1,143 (from $978) [F9].
- Payer-funded companionship is fragile: about three dozen health plans and employers did not renew Papa contracts [F6] → a family-paid model.
- Honey Homes: $9.25M Series A-1 (May 2024), $21M+ total, 1,000+ members in 2024 [F10]; reported $167–395/mo (undated press) [F11].
- **Sweden:** RUT covers 50% of labor cost for household services, including IT help in the home; ROT+RUT cap SEK 75,000/person/year [F7].

**Concept:** for adult children of homeowners 70+ in one metro. Monthly 2-hour visit from the same vetted tech-and-handyperson (safety walkthrough, small fixes, device updates), unlimited remote tech help, seasonal tasks (alarms, filters, gutters), photo report to the family. **$129/mo.**

**Risks:** state home-care licensing (avoid personal care), background checks, elder-abuse/property-damage liability, worker classification, natural churn when a parent moves or dies.

### 4.3 Neighbourhood Sauna Society — best place-based play

**Trend**
- UK public sauna sites: ~45 (2023) → 100+ (2024) → 147 (early 2025) [S1][S2].
- Eventbrite: Nordic-style wellness events +62.5% (2024 → 2025), attendance +32.8% [S3]. ClassPass: sports-recovery bookings +155% YoY [S4].
- Bathhouse (NYC) raised $35M (May 2026), targeting 10 US sites by 2028 [S6]. Perspire Sauna Studio: ~100 open, 275 licences awarded (franchisor, Sep 2025) [S8]. Global wellness economy $6.8T (2024), +7.6%/yr to 2029 [S9].

**Willingness to pay (price ladder):** Community Sauna Baths London £9.50–16.50/session [S14] → Othership NYC from $51/mo, unlimited $333/mo, drop-in $64 [S10] → Bathhouse $149/mo weekday unlimited, $189 all sites [S11] → Arc London from £125/mo for 5 sessions [S12]. **Nordic model:** Oslo Badstuforening has 18,000+ members at NOK 400/yr; sessions NOK 150 members / NOK 260 non-members; NOK 600 monthly pass [S15].

**Gap:** city-centre premium sites are crowding; mid-size cities and suburbs, and the $50–100/mo band, are under-served.

**Concept:** two modular wood-fired saunas + cold tubs on leased brewery or waterfront land (~30 people). $79/mo unlimited off-peak with 2 guest passes; $25 drop-in; an Oslo-style annual tier. Weekly hosted rituals (Aufguss, post-run sweats, women's nights) drive retention.

**Risks:** leases/permits for park or waterfront sites; heat/cold-plunge liability; energy and staffing (one UK operator cites £8–10k/month wages); summer dip; ~£40k loans per sauna [S16]. Lesson from Swedish padel (Section 5): start with one movable unit, presell founding memberships, add capacity only on waitlists.

### 4.4 Friendship cohorts ("Seasons")

**Trend:** WHO: 1 in 6 people lonely, linked to ~871,000 deaths/yr; 17–21% of 13–29-year-olds lonely [S17]. Gallup: 25% of US men aged 15–34 lonely "a lot of the day yesterday" [S18]. Timeleft: €18M ARR, 150k monthly participants (Aug 2025, founder-reported), after cutting from 320+ to 200+ cities because small cities lacked density [S22][S23]. **Counter-signal:** a dozen-plus US friendship apps earned only ~$16M combined consumer spend in 2025 to date [S24]. Soho House: members +2.2% but membership revenue +15.9% (Q2 2025) — pricing power for belonging [S25]; taken private at ~$2.7B, closed Jan 2026 after financing was rebuilt [S26][S27].

**Concept:** 12-week cohorts of 8 matched by life stage and neighbourhood; rotating formats (dinner, walk, sauna, games); keep or reshuffle each season. ~$35/mo on 3-month plans. **Verdict:** density and low revenue per user make it weak alone — use it as the community layer inside #1 or #4.

### 4.5 Also evaluated

- **Managed AI receptionist** — home-services firms miss 27% of inbound calls (41% at weekends) [D21]; but entry prices are $49–79 (Rosie $49/$149/$299 [D23]; Goodcall $79/$129/$249 [D24]; Smith.ai AI $0/$150/$500 [D25]) and trade platforms bundle it (Jobber add-on ~$99, third-party [D26]). Upsell, not a lead product.
- **Phone-free kids club** — 35+ US states + DC require school phone bans or restrictions [F12]; Sweden's national ban starts autumn 2026 [F14]; Denmark and Finland moving the same way [F15][F16]. Kids' phone hardware is crowded (Bark $29–79/mo, Gabb $24.99–34.99, Pinwheel ~$15–30 [F19]; Tin Can raised $12M seed, Dec 2025 [F20]). After-school phone-free community is open, but safeguarding/licensing risk is high.
- **Texas ESA tutoring** — 274k+ applications, ~96k awarded, $10,474 per student, $1B cap [F17]. Policy-dependent.
- **White-label vet wellness plans** — US pet spend $158B (2025) [F21]; >95% of dogs and cats uninsured [F22]; vet prices +5.4% YoY [F23]; UK CMA now requires vets to publish prices and unbundle health plans [F31]. Clinic side is consolidating (below).
- **AI-literacy memberships (EU)** — Article 4 survived the Digital Omnibus as a softer duty to "take measures to support the development of AI literacy" (Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, in force 27 Jul 2026) [D37][D38]. Urgency is lower than expected.
- **EU accessibility compliance** (not researched in depth) — the European Accessibility Act has applied since 28 Jun 2025 to e-commerce and many digital services [D40]. Audit → fixes → monitoring fits a web team; enforcement and pricing unverified.

## 6. Market & regulation → design rules

**Fatigue is about price and value, not subscriptions as such.**
- 47% of people who cancelled in 2024 cited price increases; 84% say value held or improved; 64% won't pay extra for GenAI features (Zuora/Harris — vendor) [M1].
- 41% of US consumers cancelled a paid streaming service in six months; 22% cancelled and came back within six months (Deloitte 2026, secondary) [M3].
- UK: ~155m active subscriptions; ~£1.6bn/yr spent on unwanted ones (Government, Aug 2026) [M2].

**Retention benchmarks**
- 35% of annual-plan cancellations happen in month 1; 95% of cancelled annual subscribers never return; monthly subscribers reactivate ~4× more (RevenueCat 2026 — app data) [M6][M7].
- Monthly churn: < 2% strong, 2–4% typical, > 5% investigate; 38% of consumers would rather pause than cancel (Recurly 2026, secondary; period labelling ambiguous) [M9][M10].

**Regulation (status 2 Oct 2026)**
- **US federal:** click-to-cancel rule vacated 8 Jul 2025 [M18]; FTC restarted with an ANPRM (11 Mar 2026; comments closed 13 Apr) [M20][M21]; enforcement continues under ROSCA — Amazon $2.5B settlement (Sep 2025) [M11][M12], Uber One amended complaint with 21 states + DC (Dec 2025) [M13], Chegg $7.5M [M14], Match $14M [M15], LA Fitness sued [M16].
- **US states:** California AB 2863 (1 Jul 2025): click-to-cancel, limits on save offers, annual reminders, consent records [M23]. New York (5 Nov 2025): cancel via the same channel, price rises need consent or a refundable exit [M24]. Minnesota, Massachusetts, Virginia (1 Jul 2026) similar [M25].
- **EU:** withdrawal button (Directive 2023/2673, new Art. 11a CRD) applies from 19 Jun 2026 to **all** consumer distance contracts concluded online — it serves the 14-day withdrawal right, not general cancellation [M26][M27]. Germany: §356a BGB [M28]; BGH (16 Jul 2026, I ZR 200/25) — the cancellation confirmation page may contain only the cancellation fields and button, no "pause instead" offer [M36]. Digital Fairness Act proposal expected ~11 Nov 2026 (dark patterns, subscription traps) [M29][M30].
- **UK:** DMCC Act subscription regime from **January 2027**; 14-day cooling-off after a trial converts or a long contract renews; CMA fines up to 10% of global turnover [M2][M33][M34].

**Design rules**
1. Cancel online, in the channel people joined in, in one step. Friction is now the most expensive retention tactic.
2. Offer pause/downgrade in account settings and renewal reminders — never on the cancellation confirmation page.
3. Raise prices only with consent you can prove.
4. Turn mandatory renewal reminders into value recaps (sessions attended, strength scores).
5. Win the first 30 days: book the next assessment at every visit; start members in cohorts.
6. Engineer out failed-payment churn: card updaters, smart retries, pre-expiry notices.
7. Make rejoining one click and count returners in cohort metrics.
8. Treat free trials as regulated events — explicit consent at conversion.
